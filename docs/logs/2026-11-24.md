# Engineering Log - 2026-11-24

## NextSession.md Made Short, and Its Contents Given Homes

### Purpose and scope

The home page build stamp advances to 2026-11-24 with this commit.

The owner began to read `NextSession.md` and stopped: "That document is
way, way, way too long. Next Session.md should be a very short, compact
instruction of what should be worked on next." It had 1,000 lines. It had
been cut to 102 on 2026-11-21, also at the owner's request, and grew back
in three days, because every session recorded there whatever it had
settled or finished, on the reasoning that the next session would look
there. Item 13, on Amico, was 411 lines by itself.

It held four kinds of thing, and only the first is its job: what to work
on next; decisions already made, which never expire; accounts of finished
work, which the changelog and the logs already have; and background and
method. Documents only: no code changed, apart from one comment in a test
that pointed at the old place.

### What changed

- **`NextSession.md`**, 89 lines: Next, which is tighter code for Amico
  by the owner's word; Waiting for the Owner, the decisions that are the
  owner's; the North Star, cut to two paragraphs, since `AGENTS.md` cites
  it there by name; and Open Work, one bullet to an item. The items keep
  their numbers, because the logs cite them by number.
- **`docs/amico-design.md`**, new, from item 13. Rearranged from the
  order things happened in to what a reader needs: what the owner settled
  and what stays out; what is built, with the small print the agent
  settled and the owner has not seen; what is not built, tighter code
  first; the six programs it is steered by, with their sizes; and, as
  history, the first look and the five-reviewer check of the compiler as
  Codex wrote it, moved whole, since the log of 2026-11-23 points to them
  and does not repeat them.
- **`docs/program-shelf.md`**, new, from item 1, moved whole.
- **`docs/open-work.md`**, new: items 2 to 12 and 14 and the cassette
  notes, each under its number.
- **`AGENTS.md`**: a rule in the Agent Operating Contract for where each
  kind of thing goes; How to Verify, which was a section of
  `NextSession.md`, under Testing the Emulator; and five pointers that
  named an item of `NextSession.md`. `compiler/README.md` and a comment in
  `tests/compiler.test.js` point to the new places too.

### What was dropped, and where it still is

- Where Things Stand, a list of what the machine and the page do:
  `AGENTS.md` describes all of it. What is on `develop` and not live stays,
  as a line.
- From item 2, which devices the handheld layout was tried on: `AGENTS.md`,
  Handheld Layout. From item 3, the account of the Italian's review and of
  its "noi": `AGENTS.md`, Two Languages, and the log of 2026-11-22.
- From item 13, the account of each series' review and the marks of what
  was done in which order: the log of 2026-11-23, and `AGENTS.md`, A
  Review by Several Agents. What each construct does is in
  `compiler/README.md`.
- From the North Star, that the product brief of 2026-11-22 is not in the
  repository: the log of that day.
- The count of tests, which was out of date as often as not.

The logs up to 2026-11-23 cite "`NextSession.md`, item N". They are a
record and were not changed; each new document says at its head which item
it was.
