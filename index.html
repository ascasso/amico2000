About this board

The AMICO 2000 was designed by A.S.E.L. in Milan and published as a build-it-yourself project in the magazine Sperimentare from December 1978. A MOS 6502 at 1 MHz, 1 KB of RAM, sixteen hex keys, seven function keys and six red seven-segment displays, all on one green board about the size of a keyboard.

This page runs the original monitor ROM, so entering and executing 6502 machine code works the way it did on the real hardware. The layout, silkscreen legends and keycap colours follow photographs of a finished board; the transformer, capacitors and slide switch are there for the look of the thing and do nothing.

Emulator by Andrea Scasso · original design by A.S.E.L. Milano, 1978 · board photographs on ComputerHistory.it
